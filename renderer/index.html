About Us

Welcome to The Zaffran Grill & Curries, a distinguished destination for authentic Mughlai cuisine in Meerut and Ghaziabad. Renowned as one of the finest Mughlai restaurants in the region, we take pride in presenting a culinary experience that reflects the grandeur and richness of India’s royal heritage. Our menu features an exquisite selection of succulent grills and luxurious curries, each prepared with meticulous attention to detail and the finest ingredients. Guided by traditional recipes and time-honored techniques, our chefs ensure that every dish embodies the depth, aroma, and flavor that define Mughlai gastronomy.
